# NetAudit Engine — final prototype deck

## 1. Mixed networks, fragmented assurance

- Enterprise environments combine many vendors, syntaxes and compliance frameworks.
Manual review is slow; vendor-locked tooling creates blind spots; probabilistic
AI-only auditing can produce confident but unsafe verdicts.

**SIH 26155 · NTRO · Blockchain & Cybersecurity**

## 2. One defensible security model

```text
Cisco / Fortinet / future adapters
                ↓
     normalized SecurityBaseline
                ↓
       version-controlled OPA
```

Vendor syntax changes. Security intent—SSH, Telnet, SNMP, IKE, logging, NTP,
AAA and management access—remains comparable.

## 3. AI extracts; rules decide

- Local SLM transforms text into structured facts.
- Pydantic rejects invalid shapes.
- Confidence, logprob and reverse-translation checks route uncertainty to people.
- OPA alone issues the reproducible pass/fail result.
- Raw file and normalized baseline receive independent SHA-256 fingerprints.

Speaker line: “We use AI where ambiguity exists, never where accountability is required.”

## 4. Live workflow

1. Upload `cisco_insecure.cfg`.
2. Show detected platform, confidence and file hash.
3. Open exact Telnet/SNMP/IKE evidence.
4. Generate vendor-specific remediation.
5. Approve a `SAFE` fix; demonstrate a `RISK_FLAGS` block.
6. Export the evidence report.

## 5. Safety is a product feature

- Air-gap-ready local services
- Secret redaction before durable storage
- Fail-closed evaluator dependency
- Human approval with operator attribution
- AI drafts permanently separated from approved deterministic templates
- Immutable evaluation history and machine-readable SIEM export

## 6. What the prototype proves

- Two-vendor demonstration: Cisco IOS-XE and Fortinet FortiOS
- One normalized model
- 11-control CIS-style policy subset
- Deterministic remediation with guarded approval
- PDF, JSON and CEF evidence

Do not label these results “CIS certified.”

## 7. Validation

Show only figures generated by `benchmarks/score_demo_results.py` on the

## 8. Deployment roadmap

- Validate official organizational CIS/NIST/STIG/ISO mappings
- Expand reviewed fixtures and vendor adapters
- Promote reviewed AI drafts into signed deterministic templates
- Integrate enterprise identity, WORM retention and SIEM/ticketing
- Package signed offline model and policy bundles

## Closing

**From heterogeneous configuration to explainable action—without asking AI to
decide what is secure.**
